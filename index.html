What it does while you talk

The notes were never the point.

01

Live transcript

Speech goes to text on your own machine while the meeting runs. Ask it what's happened so far without stopping the recording.

02

A summary that stands alone

Conclusion first, then decisions with the reasoning behind them and the alternative that got rejected. Someone who missed it can still follow.

03

Action items with real owners

A name appears only if that person actually took the task. Nobody gets assigned work because they happened to be talking.

04

Shares itself

Summaries land in a shared folder for everyone who attended. If a teammate recorded the same meeting, you are matched automatically.

05

Redaction that follows through

Mark a line, or ask for it in plain language. It leaves the transcript, the summary, the shared copy and the channel post at once.

06

It knows when the call ended

When the meeting window disappears, Switchboard asks whether to stop, so you never record the room after everyone has gone.
